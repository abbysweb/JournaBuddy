Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. AI Safety & No Model Training

We understand that your manuscript contains novel research and intellectual property. Our approach to AI safety is strict and transparent:

  • No Model Training: We strictly do not use your manuscripts to train, fine-tune, or improve our AI models. Your research remains yours.
  • Vendor Guarantee: We use Google Cloud's Vertex AI (Gemini) which contractually guarantees that prompts and responses are not used to train models.
  • Isolation: Your data is processed in an isolated environment and is never shared with other users or the public.

2. The Lifecycle of Your Manuscript

Unlike traditional services that store your data indefinitely, we operate on a strict retention policy designed for security:

  1. Upload & Encryption: Your file is uploaded via a secure, encrypted connection (TLS/SSL).
  2. Processing: The file is temporarily stored in volatile memory only for the duration of the AI analysis (typically 1-3 minutes).
  3. Report Generation: Once the analysis is complete, a report is generated and sent to your email.
  4. 2-Hour Access Window: The results dashboard is available for exactly 2 hours to allow you to download your feedback.
  5. Permanent Deletion: After 2 hours, the manuscript and the generated report are permanently and unrecoverably deleted from our servers.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our service:

  • Manuscript Files: Collected solely for the purpose of generating the review and deleted immediately after the retention period.
  • Email Address: Required to deliver your review report and provide customer support.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous technical data (browser type, device type) to ensure our website functions correctly.
