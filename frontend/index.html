Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

1. AI Safety & No Model Training

We strictly do not use your manuscripts to train or improve our AI models. Your research remains yours. We use Google Cloud's Vertex AI (Gemini) which contractually guarantees that prompts and responses are not used for training.

2. Manuscript Lifecycle

  1. Upload via encrypted TLS connection
  2. Temporary storage in volatile memory during analysis (1–3 min)
  3. Report generated and sent to your email
  4. Dashboard available for 2 hours
  5. Permanent unrecoverable deletion after 2 hours

3. Information Collected

  • Manuscript Files: Deleted after retention period
  • Email Address: For report delivery and support
  • Usage Data: Anonymous technical data for functionality
